Market share & summary

Realized TCGplayer marketplace GMV over ~3 months ending Jul 2026 (client-provided weights for the composite above).

Method. Fixed basket per game and segment: products priced in every sampled month, each indexed to 100 at its base month; the series is the cross-product median and the band is the 25th–75th percentile. Singles are catalog products with a rarity or collector number; everything else is sealed. Null market prices dropped; seller high prices ignored. Source: TCGplayer market prices via TCGCSV, 30 monthly snapshots (2024-02-08 – 2026-07-01). Regenerate with python3 scripts/build_dashboard.py.
